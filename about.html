About Campus Ride

Campus Ride is a reliable transportation service created to provide safe, comfortable, and affordable rides for students, residents, and visitors across Ekiti State.

We understand the challenges of moving around, especially for students who need dependable transportation for classes, events, appointments, and daily activities.

Our mission is to make transportation simple, accessible, and stress-free by connecting passengers with trusted drivers and comfortable vehicles.

Whether you're travelling within town, across Ekiti, or heading to an important destination, Campus Ride is committed to providing a safe and enjoyable journey.

Our Mission

To provide safe, affordable, and dependable transportation services that people can trust.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted transportation brand serving students and communities across Ekiti State.
